## 2. Rules by category (as stated by each source)

### 2.1 Weekday

| Category | Best | Avoid | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vehicle | Wed (AstroSight, best); Wed and Fri (Dekho); Thu for luxury (AstroSight); Mon for commercial (AstroSight) | Tue, Sat | Dekho, AstroSight |
| Property | Thu (most favourable for registration) | Sat, Tue | Dekho |
| Gold | Thu (best); Fri, Mon, Wed supportive | Tue, Sat | Astromedha |

**Conflict:** Vehicle best day is Wed (AstroSight) or Wed and Fri (Dekho). Property and gold best day is Thu.

### 2.2 Tithi

| Category | Source's list |
|---|---|
| Vehicle (Dekho) | Shukla Paksha preferred; Dwitiya, Tritiya, Panchami, Saptami, Dashami, Trayodashi favourable |
| Vehicle (AstroSight) | 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13 (best 2, 3, 5) |
| Property (Dekho) | Shukla Paksha; Dwitiya, Tritiya, Panchami, Dashami |
| Gold (Astromedha) | Shukla Paksha preferred; 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13 "clean" |

**Conflict:** Saptami is favourable in Dekho (vehicle) but not in AstroSight or gold. Tithi 13 and 7 are in some lists, not others.

### 2.3 Nakshatra

| Category | Source's list |
|---|---|
| Vehicle (Dekho) | Ashwini, Mrigashira, Pushya, Hasta, Anuradha, Revati |
| Vehicle (AstroSight) | Rohini, Mrigashira, Hasta, Pushya, Anuradha, Shravana, Revati (top: Rohini, Mrigashira, Hasta) |
| Property (Dekho) | Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada |
| Gold (Astromedha) | Pushya, Rohini, Anuradha, Revati, Uttara Phalguni |

**Conflict:** Property list (Uttara nakshatras) differs from the other two categories. Gold and vehicle lists overlap but are not the same.

### 2.4 Special days

| Category | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | Akshaya Tritiya (most celebrated); Dhanteras (Dhanvantari and Kubera worship); Guru-Pushya (Thu + Pushya) and Ravi-Pushya (Sun + Pushya) are "strongest" | Astromedha |
| Vehicle | Dhanteras as a major purchase day (dealership sales claim, no source) | AstroSight |
| Vehicle | Akshaya Tritiya overrides other rules except Rahu Kaal | AstroSight |

### 2.5 Avoided periods (common across sources)

- **Amavasya:** avoid (Astromedha, AstroSight, Dekho). Consistent.
- **Rahu Kaal:** avoid (Dekho, AstroSight). Consistent.
- **Eclipses:** avoid (Astromedha, AstroSight). Consistent.
- **Pitru Paksha, Adhik Maas:** avoid (Dekho property). Astromedha adds Kharmas (Dhanu and Meena solar transits) and Malmas. AstroSight says Kharmas and Chaturmas do **not** restrict vehicle purchase.
- **Bhadra / Vishti karana:** avoid (Astromedha, Dekho vehicle).

**Conflict:** Kharmas blocks gold (Astromedha) but not vehicle (AstroSight).

### 2.6 Lagna, house and chart checks

| Category | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Property | Saturn should not aspect 4th house on the chosen day (natal check) | Dekho |
| Gold | Venus or Jupiter in rising sign, or strong 2nd lord, no malefic aspect, Moon well placed | Astromedha |
| Vehicle | Cross-check date with buyer's lagna, dasha lord, Janma Nakshatra | AstroSight |
| Vehicle | Ascendant lord poorly placed can give malefic result | AstroSight (attributed to Muhurta Chintamani, no verse) |

These are the only chart-based rules found. They are not sourced to a verse.

### 2.7 Vastu (property only, Dekho FAQ)

- Square or rectangular plot is auspicious. Irregular, T-shaped or triangular plots avoided.
- Plot should be higher in SW and lower in NE.

## 4. What this survey means

- None of these rules is classical or verified. All are **T**.
- Sources conflict on weekday, tithi, nakshatra and Kharmas. A single output cannot say "classical" for any of them.
- Chart-based rules (lagna, 4th house, 2nd lord) are not sourced to any verse, so they stay **P** or **O**.
- The only rule set with a verifiable classical basis in our repo is Tara Bala and Chandra Bala (commit `7f2e654`).
