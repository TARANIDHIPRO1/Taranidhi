## Reliability warnings (read before using)
- The 0–100 "Tarabala" thresholds and the planet lists are NOT from a classical text or from the TaraNidhi engine. Tarabala is the 9-tara cycle (Janma, Sampat, Vipat, Kshema, Pratyari, Sadhaka, Vadha, Mitra, Ati-Mitra), not a 0–100 score. Treat all numbers as placeholders.
- Turn 1986 contains factual errors: "Oct 18 (Friday)" — 18 Oct 2026 is Sunday; "Tarabala 82", "Ganda Moola Oct 21" and "Ekadashi Oct 23" were not computed.
- No interviews or research on how to inspect/choose a new or second-hand vehicle, or on property/gold purchase procedure, were found in turns 1985–2000.
