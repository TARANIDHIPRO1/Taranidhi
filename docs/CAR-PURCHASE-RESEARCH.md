## 1. Corrections to earlier statements

- **Earlier claim:** "second-hand car ka registration number transfer ke baad wahi rehta hai."
  **Status now:** **UNVERIFIED.** The only source found (Deccan Herald, 13 Apr 2010, about a MoRTH advisory) says the buyer must apply for a new number when a car is sold without scrapping. It is an advisory, not binding, and dated. Current Vahan/state practice must be checked before this is stated to a client.
